Lore of Mondstadt

1. The Era of Storms (2,600+ Years Ago) • The Tyrant's Rule: Old Mondstadt (now Stormterror's Lair) was ruled by Decarabian, the God of Storms. He encased the city in a fierce wind barrier to "protect" his people, though it actually trapped them. • The Rebellion: A nameless bard, a tiny wind spirit (Barbatos), the knight Amos, and a red-haired warrior led a revolution to overthrow Decarabian. • The Sacrifice: The bard died in the battle. Barbatos absorbed the wind god's power, ascended to godhood, and took the form of the deceased bard to honor him.

2. The Golden Age of Freedom • Reshaping the Land: Barbatos (now Venti) blew away the snow and ice with his winds, flattened the mountains, and founded modern Mondstadt on an island in a lake. • The Divine Departure: Venti refused to rule as a tyrant. He declared Mondstadt the "Nation of Freedom" and left the humans to govern themselves, stepping into the shadows.

3. The Aristocracy & Rebellion (1,000 Years Ago) • The Corruption: In the Archon's absence, the Lawrence Clan and other nobles enslaved the common folk and gladiators. • The Return: Venti awoke to find his city in chains. He teamed up with Vennessa, a slave gladiator. • The New Order: Together, they overthrew the aristocracy. Vennessa founded the Knights of Favonius to protect the city and later ascended to Celestia as a hawk (the Falcon of the West).

4. The Cataclysm & The Dragon (500 Years Ago) • The Shadow Invasion: The alchemist Gold created mutated monsters in Khaenri'ah that attacked Teyvat. The corrupted dragon Durin assaulted Mondstadt. • The Battle of Dragonspine: Venti and his loyal dragon, Dvalin (Stormterror), defeated Durin. Durin's venomous blood seeped into Dragonspine, while Dvalin was deeply poisoned during the fight and fell into a long, agonizing slumber.

5. Present Day • Stormterror's Return: Dvalin woke up corrupted by his poison and manipulated by the Abyss Order. He began attacking Mondstadt out of a sense of betrayal. • The Traveler's Arrival: The Traveler arrived, teamed up with the Knights of Favonius, Jean, Diluc, and Venti to purify Dvalin, saving Mondstadt and restoring the Dragon of the East to freedom
