    Lore of Temple of Space

    Contains story spoilers.

    A hall in the sky

    The Temple of Space, whose old name is Segment-Domain Tau, floats in the sky above Windrest Peak. It was made by Asmoday, the Ruler of Space, as a hall to keep records of fallen civilizations such as Hyperborea, Lang-Gan, Delphi Pytho, Ochkanatlan, Remuria and Arcadia. Inside it, time stands still. Its guardians are the Domain Keepers.

    A temple that was lifted away

    Long ago the temple was linked to another temple on Windrest Peak, where survivors of Arcadia worshipped Istaroth, the goddess of time. After a disaster the Temple of Space was lifted into the heavens, hidden from the world. The ruins left on the mountain were later rebuilt as a sanatorium.

    The temple loses its order

    At some point Asmoday disappeared from the temple. Over time the temple's laws began to fail, the border between the temple and Teyvat weakened, and Domain Keepers fell onto Windrest Peak. One of the temple's rulers, Melanta, took control of it as a tyrant.

    The Traveler arrives

    The sisters Nihilita and Zeitlind, and the young Watcher Dex, call on the Traveler to free the temple. Inside they meet Istoria, a woman who lives in the temple. Together they enter the Apathic Interval, where people lifted to the temple must give up their desires, and they work to free the temple and its people.
