    Lore of Inazuma

    Contains story spoilers.

    The island nation of eternity

    Inazuma is an island nation in the east of Teyvat, known for its storms, its shrines and its cherry blossoms. Its Archon is Beelzebul, the Electro god, who rules as the Raiden Shogun. Her ideal is Eternity: she wants Inazuma to stay unchanged forever. The government is the Inazuma Shogunate, and its work is split between the Kanjou, Tenryou and Yashiro Commissions.

    The Vision Hunt Decree

    Five hundred years ago the Cataclysm took the life of Ei's twin sister Makoto. Ei, who was grieving, set out to build an eternity where nothing could be lost again. She closed the country's borders with the Sakoku Decree. She also began the Vision Hunt Decree, which takes Visions from people because she believed their ambition leads to change and loss. The Kujou Clan enforced the orders, and the Kamisato Clan helped run the state.

    The puppet Shogun and the Resistance

    Ei withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia and left a puppet Shogun to carry out her will. On Watatsumi Island, Sangonomiya Kokomi led a Resistance against the Vision Hunt, and the Traveler joined the people of Inazuma against the decree. At the end, Ei came to see that eternity and change do not have to oppose each other, and the Vision Hunt Decree was ended.

    Islands and places

    Narukami Island holds the Grand Narukami Shrine and its keeper Yae Miko, and Inazuma City is the capital. Yashiori Island is where the Tatara smiths and the Arataki Gang live. Watatsumi Island is Kokomi's home, and Seirai Island is troubled by constant lightning. Beneath the waves lies Enkanomiya, a sunken realm with its own long history.
