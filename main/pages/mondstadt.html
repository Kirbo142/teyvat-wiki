    Lore of Mondstadt

    Contains story spoilers.

    The city of freedom

    Mondstadt is the nation of freedom, in the northwest of Teyvat, with its cliffs, lakes and wide green hills. Its Archon is Barbatos, the god of wind, but no god rules the city today. The Knights of Favonius protect it. Jean serves as Acting Grand Master while Grand Master Varka is away, and the Knights include Cavalry Captain Kaeya and Chief Alchemist Albedo. Bards sing in the streets, and the city celebrates the wind at the Windblume Festival.

    Tyranny and revolt

    Long ago Mondstadt lived under Decarabian, a cruel ruler whose tower stood over the land. The people were oppressed until a woman named Vennessa rose against him, with the help of Barbatos. Decarabian fell, and Barbatos made a choice that shaped the nation. He gave the people their freedom and stepped back, so Mondstadt has had no ruler since.

    Barbatos among the people

    Barbatos never left. He lives among Mondstadt's people as Venti, a bard who loves wine and songs. Few in the city know who he really is.

    The Wind Dragon

    Dvalin, also called Stormterror, was once a close friend of Barbatos. The poison of the Abyss twisted him, and his storms now threaten the city. The Traveler, the Knights and Venti work together to free Dvalin from the poison, and this fight opens the story of Teyvat.

    Places in Mondstadt

    The Cathedral of Favonius stands at the heart of the city. Dawn Winery sits near Cider Lake, and the great tree of Windrise watches over the road. To the north lie the frozen mountains of Dragonspine, home to ruins of an old civilization, and the quiet heights of Windrest Peak, where a path leads up to the Temple of Space.
