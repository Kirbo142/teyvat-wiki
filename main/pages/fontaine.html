    Lore of Fontaine

    Contains story spoilers.

    The nation of justice

    Fontaine is the nation of justice, in the north-central part of Teyvat. Its capital, the Court of Fontaine, is a city of canals and elegant buildings, and its people are humans and Melusines. The government is the Palais Mermonia. Trials in Fontaine are public shows held in the grand Opera Epiclese. Anyone who can control an element can breathe underwater here.

    The prophecy

    An old prophecy says that the Primordial Sea will rise and wash away all of Fontaine's people. The Hydro Archon Focalors ruled the nation, and the people believed their time was running out. Over the centuries the Fontaine Research Institute, the courts and the Melusines each worked in their own way to understand what the waters meant.

    Furina and Focalors

    For about five hundred years the Archon appeared to the people as Furina, who played her role on the stage of Fontaine's courts. The truth is that Focalors split her divinity from her human half. The human Furina played the Archon to hide the plan from the people, and from the prophecy itself. After the events of the story Focalors is gone, and Furina lives as an ordinary person.

    The Chief Justice

    Neuvillette, the Iudex of Fontaine, hides a secret: he is the Hydro Dragon Sovereign, a being tied to the waters of the nation. The fortress of Meropide, ruled by Duke Wriothesley, lies under the lake, and the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, an old order of knights, has its own part in Fontaine's history.

    The Sea of Bygone Eras

    Deeper in the waters of Fontaine lies the Sea of Bygone Eras, a sunken region of ancient ruins that opens up in later quests.
