    Lore of The Chasm

    Contains story spoilers.

    A mining district

    The Chasm lies in Liyue, beside Lingju Pass and Qingxu Pool, west of Liyue Harbor. It was once one of Liyue's largest sources of ores and precious stones. After a recent event the Liyue Qixing closed the mines, and they stayed shut until version 2.6.

    The Underground Mines

    Below the surface lies The Chasm: Underground Mines, an area with its own map and its own exploration progress. It is dark, so you use the Lumenstone Adjuvant to light the way. The technician Jinwu can improve it, and the Ruin Serpent waits in the Serpent's Cave as the area's boss.

    In the story

    The Archon Quest Requiem of the Echoing Depths takes the Traveler down into the mines. At the mining camp, Yuehui gives the next objective, and Lan starts the Chasm Delvers quests beside the Adventurers' Guild. The Lost Valley domain lies at Fuao Vale on the surface.
