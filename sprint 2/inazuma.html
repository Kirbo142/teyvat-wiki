Lore of Inazuma

Inazuma is a chain of islands in the far east of Teyvat, shaped by an ancient flood that left only a few islands above the water. Thousands of years later, the twin sisters Raiden Makoto and Raiden Ei fought together in the Archon War. Ei united the islands, then gave up her own body so that Makoto could become the Electro Archon, and she lived on as her sister's shadow double.

Ei later fought the serpent god Orobashi of Watatsumi Island. When her friend Sasayuri fell in battle, she killed him with a single blow and split Yashiori Island in two. His hatred lived on as the Tatarigami, which the Shogunate has been holding back ever since.

About five hundred years ago, Makoto died during the fall of Khaenri'ah. Heartbroken, Ei became obsessed with an unchanging "eternity" where nothing can be lost. She made a puppet, the Raiden Shogun, to rule for her, and withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia.

Not long before the Traveler arrives, the Shogun closed the borders with the Sakoku Decree and seized people's Visions with the Vision Hunt Decree. A civil war followed. With the help of Ayaka, Thoma and Yae Miko, the Traveler convinces Ei to end the Vision Hunt, and Inazuma slowly begins to heal and open up to the world again.

Shops in Inazuma

  • Kiminami Restaurant

    It’s across from the Amenoma Smithy in Hanamizaka and is owned by Kiminami Anna, who sells recipes. She is also the quest giver for Pizza From Another Land.

  • Shimura’s

    A small restaurant across from the Adventurers’ Guild in Inazuma. It also sells recipes.

  • Electro Sigil Shop

    The Inazuma counterpart of Mondstadt’s and Liyue’s sigil shops, found in Inazuma City

  • Amenoma Smithy

    A smithy on the eastern end of Inazuma City, in Hanamizaka.

  • Yae Publishing House

    A giant of the Inazuma publishing industry, founded by Yae Miko, in Hanamizaka.
