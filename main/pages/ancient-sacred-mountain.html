    Lore of Ancient Sacred Mountain

    Contains story spoilers.

    The Sanctified Land

    The Ancient Sacred Mountain is an area of Natlan, added in version 5.5. It lies in Atocpan, in the southwest of Natlan, southwest of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. The area has its own map, and it holds the Great Volcano of Tollan and the Sacred City of Tollan, also known as the Crumbling City.

    The Traveler's Saurian

    The quests here continue the Traveler's journey to remove the Flamelord's Blessing from their little Saurian friend. The chain Chronicler of the Crumbling City leads through the volcano to the Celestial Axis in the Sacred City.

    Exploring the mountain

    You get in by finishing Investigator of Ancient Ruins, then diving into the Spiritway during Nahuatzin's Leap. Inside, dragon puzzles, fireballs, falling volcanic rocks and Phlogiston vents stand in the way. Qucusaurs help you move around, and pulverite blocks can be broken to reach hidden places.
