    Lore of Sea of Bygone Eras

    Contains story spoilers.

    A sunken kingdom

    The Sea of Bygone Eras is an underwater area of Fontaine, added in version 4.6. It has its own map outside the main map, and its entrance lies in the Nostoi region on the south side of Fontaine. In this area lies Remuria, a kingdom that existed in Fontaine long before the reign of the previous Hydro Archon, Egeria.

    Scylla

    The former prince of Remuria now lives on as a whale-like being named Scylla. He helps you move through the area and appears near wherever you swim. The halls of the fallen kingdom are guarded by Praetorian Golems, statues in the shape of warriors that attack anyone who comes too close.

    How to get there

    The area opens through the world quest chain Canticles of Harmony. Start by activating the Statue of the Seven at Petrichor to light up the Nostoi region, then follow the quests until the sea opens up.

    Things to find

    The area has 46 Hydroculi, several treasure chests and puzzles, and a Shrine of Depths. It is also where you can fight the weekly boss The Knave, which is a battle against Arlecchino.
